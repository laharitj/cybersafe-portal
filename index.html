India · financial cyber fraud

Report fraud promptly

Contact your bank through verified channels and report financial cyber fraud using the national helpline.

01

Contact your bank

Use the phone number or support option in your official banking app, card, or bank website. Do not rely on details in a suspicious message.

02

National cyber fraud helpline

1930

In India, call 1930 promptly to report suspected financial cyber fraud.

03

Official reporting portal

Submit a cybercrime report through the Government of India's official portal.
